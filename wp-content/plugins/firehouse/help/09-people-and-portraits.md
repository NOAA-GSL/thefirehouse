# People and portraits

<!-- help audience: administrators; screens: -->

How to give someone the FireHouse Editor role, and how to add a portrait for the About page's team.

## Give someone the FireHouse Editor role

1. Go to **Users**, and open the person's profile, or choose **Add New User** for someone new.
2. Under **Role**, choose **FireHouse Editor**.
3. Choose **Update User** (or **Add New User**).

A FireHouse Editor can change the FireHouse and nothing else on the site: the projects, topic areas, synthesis, settings, and the words on the landing and About pages. They can't install plugins, import, change other pages or menus, add or remove pages, or upload images.

## Add a portrait to the About page

FireHouse Editors choose team members' portraits from the images attached to the FireHouse pages. They can't upload images, so an administrator adds a new portrait first:

1. Go to **Media → Add New Media File** and upload the photo: portrait-shaped, 4 wide by 5 high, and at least 336 by 420 pixels.
2. Attach it to the About page: in **Media → Library**, switch to the list view, and in the photo's **Uploaded to** column choose **Attach**, then the About page.
3. Tell the editor. In the block editor, the team member's **Portrait** now offers it.

## Remove someone

To take away the role, open the person's profile under **Users**, and choose another role, or "No role for this site". Their projects' and pages' revisions stay.
